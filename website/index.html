FAQ

What is CatXapp?

An iPhone app for scrap yards and catalytic converter buyers — code search, live PGM-adjusted buy prices, carts, margin, and PDF receipts.

How do prices update?

Catalog prices adjust with live platinum, palladium, and rhodium spots. Tap refresh on the market strip for the latest.

Essential vs Pro?

Free: 5 lookups per week. Essential: unlimited lookup + live PGM prices. Pro: cart, margin, saved loads, and PDF export.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Download free and get 5 code lookups per week with no account. Pro monthly also includes a 3-day free trial via the App Store (previously 14 days). Cancel in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.
