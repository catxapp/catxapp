FAQ

What is CatXapp?

An iPhone app for professional buyers. Look up a full code or the part you can still read, see live metal prices, and on Pro save clients, send a signed invoice, and chat with a real person.

How do prices update?

Catalog prices adjust with live platinum, palladium, and rhodium spots. Tap refresh on the market strip for the latest.

Essential vs Pro?

Free: 10 lookups a week. Essential: unlimited lookup and live metal prices. Pro: cart, margin, saved loads, clients, one-page invoices with a finger signature, and live chat with a real person. You can see Cart and Clients on any plan. Pro is what lets you use them.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Download free and get 10 code lookups per week with no account. Pro monthly also includes a 7-day free trial via the App Store. Cancel in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.

Where do I find the converter code?

On the canister first — it may hide under the heat shield. Read our guide: where to find the code on a catalytic converter (and how to read a rusty one).

What if the converter has no code?

On Pro, tap + in the cart and type a brand, price, and how many. If you leave the brand blank, the cart shows Unknown. These rows keep the price you typed.

What is on the invoice?

One page. Your company, the seller, how many catalytic converters, and the total price. Not a list of every code. You can add a finger signature, preview it before saving, and open it again from Invoices.

What is live chat?

On Pro, open Settings → About → Telegram. A real person answers, not a robot. Please be polite. No insults, no spam, and no rude messages.

Android?

iPhone only for now — requires iOS 17 or later.
